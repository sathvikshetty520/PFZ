Harmonization Principles Implemented:

  • Dual-Domain Architecture: Provides both the complete North Indian Ocean basin (with explicit SSH sub-domain mask) and the coincident sub-domain where 100% of all variables overlap.
  • Temporal Synchrony: Daily matching by standard calendar date (YYYY-MM-DD); hourly ERA5 wind aggregated to daily mean and wind speed magnitude.
  • Monthly Climatological Matching: Monthly mean resampled datasets across 24 calendar months (2020-01 to 2021-12) for seasonal modeling and cloud gap mitigation.
  • High-Speed Columnar Parquet: High-performance tabular outputs generated alongside CF-1.8 NetCDF4 files for machine learning pipelines.
